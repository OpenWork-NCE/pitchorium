## Commitment

Pitchorium aims at level AA of the WCAG 2.2 guidelines.

## Means in place

_Means verified on 12 October 2026, to be confirmed by the independent audit._

- Automated audit (axe, WCAG 2.2 AA) of every screen of the site and of the application, in the light and dark themes, at each nightly verification; audit of every component of the design system in both themes.
- Colour contrast checked by tests on every pair of text and background colours.
- A complete journey, from a first visit to the deletion of the account, made with the keyboard alone, without a mouse: sign-up, contribution, messaging, report, personal data.
- Visible focus, skip links, order of the headings, accessible names and announcements of the live regions checked by the tests of the journeys.
- Layout checked on a computer and on a phone, in French and in English, and with texts 40 % longer: no text cut or drawn over another, no page wider than the screen.
- The reduced motion preference is honoured: each animation shows its final state at once.
- Lighthouse audits of the public pages, with an expected accessibility score of 100.
