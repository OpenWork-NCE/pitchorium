## What can be reported

A profile, an organisation, a publication, a comment, a project and its updates, an event, a mission, a message or a file. Each report gives a reason: spam, harassment, fraud, illegal content, misleading information, intellectual property or other.

## Handling

The reports about the same content are gathered in a moderation case. A decision can hide or remove the content, warn or suspend a member, or stop the contributions of a project; it rests on the terms of use or on the law.

## Information of the people concerned

The person who reports is told that the report was received, then of its outcome. The member concerned by a decision receives its statement of reasons and can appeal.
