## What Pitchorium stores

Pitchorium uses no advertising cookie and no audience measurement tracker that requires consent. No consent banner is therefore shown. The trackers below are strictly necessary to the service or answer a request of the person.

- **Session** (`pitchorium.session_token`): keeps a member signed in. Removed at sign-out.
- **Language** (`NEXT_LOCALE`): keeps the chosen language, one year.
- **Time zone** (`pitchorium.tz`): shows times in the zone of the reader, one year.
- **Theme** (local storage of the browser): keeps the chosen light or dark theme.
- **Drafts and pending sends** (local storage and IndexedDB): keep on the device a draft of a publication or a message and the sends made offline; erased at publication, at sending, at sign-out and after 24 hours for the sends.
- **Recent searches** (local storage): erased at sign-out.

## Third-party services

- **Audience and performance measurement** (Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights, when enabled): no cookie, no identifier stored on the device.
- **Error tracking** (Sentry, when configured): no cookie, no headers, no user data.
- **Bot protection** (Cloudflare Turnstile, on the sign-in and sign-up forms): necessary to the security of the service.
- **Project videos** (YouTube in its no-cookie variant, Vimeo with `dnt=1`): nothing is loaded before a click on the video.
